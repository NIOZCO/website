Origin of the Idea

The basic idea behind the logo originated from the letter "N".

Both my first and last name end with the letter "N", which made this letter the central basis for the entire design.

Additionally, Braille served as creative inspiration for the initial forms and structures.

From this idea, a unique visual concept developed step by step.
