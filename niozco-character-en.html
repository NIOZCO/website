Creation of the character

Here you will find more information about the creation of my character and how I developed it.

Bildquelle: imgbin.com

Inspiration: Mask from Haku in Naruto

Own further development, own logo, own character idea and post-processing.

First, I drew my own mask.

Then I made a digital version of it.

From the mask and the own logo, I created my own Minecraft skin.

Subsequently, I used ChatGPT to create a character visualization based on my own designs.

The generated image was then edited and adjusted in GIMP.

Here is another comparison between the generated and edited versions.

From this, the final figure "NIOZCO" was created.
