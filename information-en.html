About me

I'm a creative professional who enjoys drawing, developing small programs, and working on digital projects.

This website is my personal space to develop my own ideas and projects step by step—without any time pressure, purely for the joy of creating.

I work independently, on a small scale, and without any commercial intent. All content is created in my free time—as a long-term passion project.

Note on AI usage

In a time when AI can generate text, images, and music at the touch of a button, I consciously choose a different path.

I use artificial intelligence exclusively as a support tool—not as a replacement for creative work.

My projects are primarily created through my own work:

  • Drawings—analog or digital

  • Self-programmed tools and websites

  • Original designs and ideas

  • Image editing without AI filters

AI can occasionally provide support, for example:

  • Brainstorming

  • Considering alternatives

  • Addressing technical questions

However, the creative result always originates from me or is the result of human collaboration.

If AI was involved in creating content, this will be clearly indicated.

Exception to AI Usage

In some cases, I also use AI for quick visualizations, concept art, or initial sketches. This is primarily done to reduce significant time investment and high costs for early prototypes—especially when dealing with many variants or ideas.

However, the goal is not to replace human art or my own drawing. My own designs, characters, logos, edits, and creative decisions still come from me or are the result of human collaboration.

AI serves here as a supportive tool for specific tasks. If AI was involved in creating content, this will be transparently indicated.

Why so little AI?

AI is based on existing data, works, and ideas.

As a result, many results cannot be clearly categorized as my own work.

Therefore, I consciously choose to create the majority of my content myself.

This ensures that my projects remain:

  • Personal

  • Creative

  • Traceable

In this way, they retain their unique character.
