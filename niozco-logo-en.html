Origin of the Logo

Here you can learn more about the creation of my logo and how I developed it.

Inspiration from Braille

My first name and last name both end with the letter N.

From two instances of the letter N, I then created a capital N with two N's hidden inside.

After that, I made several versions of it and in the end chose two that I liked the most.

From these two versions, I then made new versions, from which I finally found a version that I like.

After that, I made a square sketch and created a digital version from it.

That's how the logo was created.

Here I have the logo again in different colors and color combinations.
