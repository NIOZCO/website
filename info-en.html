1. About Me

I enjoy working on drawings, small programs, and various projects.

In the process, I generate original ideas, creative concepts, and personal works, which I continue to develop step by step.

All projects are created independently and primarily in my spare time.

The focus lies on creativity and the long-term development of my own content—without time pressure and without a commercial orientation.

2. About This Website

This website serves as a central platform for my projects, developments, and creative works.

Various types of content—such as tools, drawings, projects, and much more—are published here.

The website was programmed by me personally and is continuously being developed further.

The source code for this website is publicly available on GitHub: github.com

For specific steps in the workflow, supportive AI tools are occasionally utilized—for instance, for brainstorming or technical assistance.

However, the implementation, design, and ongoing development are primarily driven by my own work and practical experience.

3. AI

3.1 Stance on AI

I view artificial intelligence as a supportive tool, not as a substitute for creative work.

It can assist in structuring ideas more quickly, understanding technical challenges, or visualizing initial concepts.

However, the creative process, the design, and the actual decision-making emerge consciously through my own work and personal development.

3.2 Use of AI

AI is occasionally used on this website for support purposes—for instance, during brainstorming, when addressing technical questions, or in the creation of early concept drafts.

In certain instances, rapid visualizations or initial prototypes may also be generated with the aid of AI to facilitate the efficient comparison and further development of various ideas.

3.3 Transparency Regarding AI Content

Transparency is of particular importance to me when working with AI.

Therefore, content involving AI is—whenever possible—appropriately labeled.

My own creative work, human collaboration, and personal decisions remain the foundation of all projects and content on this website.
